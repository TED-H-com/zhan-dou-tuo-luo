The core idea核心觀念

Spinning longest is not winning.

轉最久，不一定最強。

The better question: under the current rules, how do three tops score most efficiently? A stamina top that mostly wins by Spin Finish earns 1 point at a time, so it has to win several times in a row to close a match. An attacker can change the whole match with one big finish, but it is also the most likely to knock itself out.

更好的問法是：在現在的規則下，怎麼用三顆陀螺把分數拿得最有效率？持久型主要靠自旋勝利，一次只拿 1 分，要連續成功好幾次才收得掉一場；攻擊型抓到一次大機會就能改變整場，但也最容易自爆。

Don't ask which top is strongest. Ask what kind of points this top earns in your deck.
不要問「哪顆最強」，
改問「這顆在我的牌組裡負責賺哪一種分數」。

The 3v3 deck3v3 牌組思路

Three tops, three jobs.

三顆陀螺，三種分工。

In the formal 3v3 format each player brings three different tops, and parts cannot repeat. So the notes treat a deck as a set of roles, not a ranking.

3v3 正式配置要帶三顆不同的陀螺，零件不可重複。所以筆記把牌組看成分工，而不是排行榜。

01

The point-grabber

搶分手

Goes for Over, Burst, and Xtreme Finishes to open up the score in one hit. The risk is a self-KO, so do not go all-in when you are already ahead.

負責 Over Finish、Burst Finish、Xtreme Finish，一次把分差拉開。風險是自爆，比分領先時不要無腦梭哈。

Phoenix Wing · Dran Buster
02

The anchor

穩守手

Cuts down self-KOs, holds the center, and makes the opponent's attacks miss. The risk is slow scoring, since a Spin Finish is usually worth only 1 point.

負責減少自爆、守中心、逼對手攻擊失誤。風險是拿分慢，因為自旋勝利通常只有 1 分。

Wizard Rod · Silver Wolf
03

The closer or counter

收尾 / 反制手

Built against what your local scene plays most, such as right-spin stamina, attackers, or Wizard Rod types. It can also take the last point safely.

針對當地常見牌組，例如專打右迴持久、抗攻擊，或處理 Wizard Rod 類型；也可以用來穩穩補最後 1 分。
